Writing Things Down for No One

I'm writing things down for no one to read. I'm taking them, putting them out into the void, like a crumpled up note. My brain never stops thinking — it's not glamorous, nor is it fun. It's like a machine gun with the trigger being held down. It's overkill. It's good for me to collect these thoughts, organize them, "publish" them and move on.

This isn't trying to be profound or anything. I don't really think we need any more of that right now in 2026.

I know there are Substacks, and I might post some of these writings there, but I've become very turned off by Substack and everyone wanting their writing to be behind a paywall. First off — is what you're saying really that great that I need to pay $5 a month to read it? I am a child of the Tumblr era where people would write basically novellas of their thoughts for free.

In a society where everything is being so curated on specific platforms, driven by an algorithm, with a fee always attached — I wanted to do the complete opposite. I think I could just also be nostalgic for the era of blogs, where we cared more about the writing, and less about the curation.

To even further hunker down on this, I am teaching myself how to code HTML and CSS as I go down this journey. As someone who has pretty severe dyslexia, it's been fun to say the least. Brace for impact.

That's it for post #1. I know you're just so captivated, and can't wait for what else is in store.
